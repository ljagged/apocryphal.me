title: "Re-reading Dennett"
---

Re-reading *Consciousness Explained* twenty years later. The computational metaphors feel quaint now — but the core argument about heterophenomenology is more relevant than ever in the age of LLMs claiming sentience. Dennett's point wasn't that consciousness is "just" computation. It was that we should study what people *say* about their experience before theorizing about what experience *is*. We're making exactly the inverse mistake with language models: theorizing wildly about what they experience based on what they say.
